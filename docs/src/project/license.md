## Trademark notice

pgmeter ships workloads (PGM-B, PGM-C, PGM-H, PGM-DS, PGM-IoT) **derived
from** the corresponding TPC and TPCx benchmark specifications. They implement
a representative subset of each specification for relative comparison (for
example, comparing two table access methods under the same workload). **They
are not audited, full-specification TPC benchmarks, and their results must
not be compared with, or represented as, official TPC results.**
